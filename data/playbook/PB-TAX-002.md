## When this applies

The letter states an amount due and a due date, and it is the first demand for this
assessment, not a final notice. Typical wording is avis de cotisation, notice of
assessment, or solde dû.

## Steps

1. Note the reference number and the due date.
2. Confirm the amount against the assessment notice already on file.
3. Check the payment destination against the registry. A demand that names an account
   we do not hold is a stop, not a payment.
4. Queue the payment for the owner. Include the reference number, the amount, the
   currency and the due date.
5. Pay at least three business days before the due date so a bank delay does not create
   interest.
6. File the confirmation with the reference number.

## If you cannot settle it

If the amount does not match our records, or the destination is unfamiliar, escalate
instead of paying.
