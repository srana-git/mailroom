## When this applies

A supplier, a carrier or a municipal office announces reduced hours or a closure for
holidays. The letter asks for nothing.

## Steps

1. Note the closed dates and the reopening date.
2. Check whether an order or a delivery falls inside the closure.
3. If it does, move the order earlier or tell the owner the new date.
4. Diary the reopening date.
5. File the notice with the supplier file.
