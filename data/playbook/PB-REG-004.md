## When this applies

A government body confirms a change of address, or asks us to confirm the address it
holds for the company. This is often a confirmation, not a demand.

## Steps

1. Compare the address in the letter with the address we actually use.
2. If they match, confirm and file. No action beyond the confirmation.
3. If they differ, find out which body still holds the old address.
4. Queue the update for the owner.
5. Send the confirmation only to the address printed on the letter, and only when that
   address matches the registry entry in our own records.
6. Record the new address in the ledger so the next letter is matched correctly.

## If you cannot settle it

If a letter asks us to confirm by clicking a link or by replying with documents,
escalate. Official confirmations arrive by post or through a portal we already use.
