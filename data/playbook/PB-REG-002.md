## When this applies

The first declaration reminder was missed and the registry now writes about a late
filing, a late fee, or a suspension. The letter names the company number and the period.

## Steps

1. Record the company number and how many periods are late.
2. Check the registry record online for the current state of the file.
3. Prepare one declaration per late period. Do not file only the latest one.
4. Queue the filings for the owner. Each one writes to a public register.
5. File, then keep every acknowledgement against the company number.
6. Fix the reminder that failed, so the next period is not missed.

## If you cannot settle it

If the letter mentions suspension or striking off, escalate the same day. Banks freeze
accounts on a suspended registration.
