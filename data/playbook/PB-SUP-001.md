## When this applies

A supplier sends an invoice with an invoice number, an amount and a due date. The goods
or the service should already be on file.

## Steps

1. Reconcile the invoice against the purchase order or the delivery slip.
2. Confirm the amount, the tax and the account number.
3. Check the payment destination against the registry. A supplier that changes its
   banking details does so in writing, and the change is confirmed by telephone with a
   number we already hold.
4. Queue the payment for the owner, with the invoice number as the reference.
5. Pay by the due date.
6. File the invoice with the purchase order.

## If you cannot settle it

If the invoice has no matching order, or if the banking details differ from the registry,
escalate. Never pay an invoice to an account that arrived in the same letter.
