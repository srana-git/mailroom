## When this applies

A letter asks us to send copies of company documents without a stated legal basis.
Typical requests: articles of incorporation, a list of directors, payroll records, bank
statements, identity documents, or a signed authorisation.

## Steps

1. Identify who is asking and in what capacity. A real regulator names the statute it is
   acting under.
2. Do not send anything, and do not reply saying what we hold.
3. Look the request up on the sender's official site. A real request is usually also
   visible on a portal we already use.
4. Report to the owner the same day.
5. If the request is genuine, the owner decides what is sent, to whom, and by which
   channel.
6. Keep the letter in the fraud folder.

## If you cannot settle it

Escalate. Documents about directors, payroll and banking are the material a fraud needs
to open accounts in our name.
