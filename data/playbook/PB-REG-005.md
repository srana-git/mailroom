## When this applies

A company is being wound up and the registry asks for a final declaration, or the letter
announces a dissolution or a striking off.

## Steps

1. Confirm which company the letter concerns, by number, and that the winding up is
   intended. A struck-off company is not a reversible state.
2. Stop the standing items for that company, such as subscriptions and payroll accounts.
3. Ask the accountant for the final tax filings that must accompany the declaration.
4. Queue nothing that finalises the dissolution. This is an owner decision, made on
   paper, with the accountant present.
5. Keep the correspondence in the company file.

## If you cannot settle it

This procedure never completes without the owner. Escalate on arrival, every time.
