## When this applies

A director, officer or shareholder has changed, or the registered address has moved, and
the registry has sent a form, a reminder or a confirmation request.

## Steps

1. Identify exactly what changed and the date of the change.
2. Confirm the new information with the owner in writing. Registry entries are public.
3. Prepare the updating declaration.
4. Queue it for the owner. Nobody is added to or removed from a public register without
   the owner's approval.
5. File within the 15 day window and keep the acknowledgement.
6. Tell the bank and the accountant if the change touches signing authority.

## If you cannot settle it

If the letter disputes a change we did not make, escalate. A third party may be using
the company number.
