## When this applies

The agency says the payroll withholding remitted for a period does not match the returns
filed, or the return filed does not match the pay records.

The same letter goes by several names. The remittance is a *remise des retenues* or a
*remise sur la paie*; "the withholding received does not match the return filed" is the same
sentence as "l'écart de remise constaté". The amount is called a *shortfall*, an *écart* or
the *balance owing* on the remittance. A letter about pay also names a *relevé d'emploi*, a
Record of Employment. Whenever the letter is about the remittance or the payroll account,
this is the procedure to open, not the general payment procedure.

## Steps

1. Pull the payroll register for the period named.
2. Sum the source deductions actually withheld.
3. Compare with the amount remitted and with the amount on the return. There are three
   numbers, and the letter usually compares only two.
4. Identify the difference. A timing difference at a month end is common and is
   explained, not corrected.
5. If we under-remitted, queue the correction and the payment together for the owner.
6. Record the reconciled figures with the period.

## If you cannot settle it

If the difference repeats for two or more periods, escalate. It usually means the
payroll setup is wrong, not the arithmetic.
