## When this applies

An assessment is wrong, or we disagree with it, and the letter is not yet final. The
letter mentions a right to object, contester, or a delay of objection.

## Steps

1. Record the deadline and the reference number before anything else.
2. List the specific lines that are wrong, with the document that proves each one.
3. Ask the accountant to confirm the position in writing.
4. Queue the notice of objection for the owner to approve. The objection is sent only
   after the owner approves it.
5. Keep proof of sending. The date of sending is what the file turns on.
6. Diarise the answer date. An unanswered objection should be chased, not forgotten.

## If you cannot settle it

If the deadline is inside five business days, escalate immediately. A late objection is
a different and much weaker procedure.
