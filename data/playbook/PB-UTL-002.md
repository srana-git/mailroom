## When this applies

A utility warns that service will be cut on a stated date because of an unpaid balance.
The letter names the account, the balance and the date.

## Steps

1. Read the disconnection date first, then everything else.
2. Check whether the balance is genuinely unpaid. A payment made at a bank can take two
   business days to appear and is not lost.
3. If the balance is real, queue the payment for the owner with the disconnection date in
   the payload.
4. If the balance is not real, queue a written challenge and send the payment proof.
5. Diary the disconnection date and check the account state on that morning.
6. Tell the site manager if the account serves a working location.

## If you cannot settle it

Escalate the same day. A disconnection stops the work, and a reconnection carries a fee
and a delay.
