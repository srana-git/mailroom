## When this applies

The workplace safety body issues an annual premium notice or a rate adjustment, naming
the employer account and the assessment year.

## Steps

1. Check the employer account number and the assessment year against our records.
2. Compare the reported payroll with the payroll actually paid in that year. The premium
   is calculated on reported payroll.
3. If the reported payroll is too high, gather the payroll summaries for the year.
4. Queue the payment, or the adjustment request, for the owner. A wrong payroll figure
   should be corrected rather than paid.
5. Keep the notice with the year's payroll file.

## If you cannot settle it

If the notice names a rate class that does not match the work we do, escalate. The rate
class follows the business for years.
