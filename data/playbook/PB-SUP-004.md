## When this applies

A supplier writes that an order will be late, or that a back-ordered item has no date
yet. The letter usually names the order or the purchase order number.

## Steps

1. Confirm the order number and what is delayed.
2. Check whether a customer commitment depends on that order.
3. If a customer date is at risk, tell the owner the same day.
4. Diary the promised date, plus three days.
5. Re-order elsewhere only if the owner agrees. A second order is a second cost.
6. File the notice with the purchase order.

## If you cannot settle it

A delay that puts a customer deadline at risk escalates, because the decision is a
commercial one.
