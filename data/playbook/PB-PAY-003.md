## When this applies

The agency reviews the payroll withholding account and asks us to confirm the number of
employees, the remittance frequency, or the account status. It reads like a
questionnaire rather than a demand.

In French this is a *révision du compte de retenues*, or a *demande de renseignements sur
le compte de retenues à la source*. It asks questions about the payroll account and does
not ask for money, so it is this procedure and not a payment procedure.

## Steps

1. Read every question and answer only what is asked.
2. Take the employee count from the payroll system for the date the letter names, not
   from memory.
3. Confirm the remittance frequency matches what we actually do. A mismatch here is the
   usual cause of a later discrepancy letter.
4. Queue the reply for the owner.
5. Send the reply and keep a copy with the account number.

## If you cannot settle it

If the review asks for employee names, social insurance numbers or banking details,
escalate before sending anything. Official reviews ask for counts and account numbers,
not for personal records.
