## When this applies

A court or an agency orders a deduction from an employee's pay, or an agency seizes an
amount from our account. The letter names a case number.

## Steps

1. Read the letter before payroll closes. A garnishment applies from the effective date,
   not from when we get round to it.
2. Confirm the case number and the effective date.
3. Do not change any payroll until the owner and the accountant have seen the letter.
   An incorrect deduction creates a liability towards the employee.
4. Send the letter to the owner the same day.
5. Record the case number in the payroll file, restricted to payroll only.

## If you cannot settle it

Always escalate. This procedure has no automatic path: it is legal, it affects an
employee, and it carries a deadline set by a court.
