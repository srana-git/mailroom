## When this applies

The letter says final notice, dernier avis, mise en demeure, or warns of collection,
seizure or a certificate filed with the court. It usually repeats an assessment that
was already sent and unpaid.

## Steps

1. Read the letter the day it arrives. Do not batch final notices with the weekly mail.
2. Find the earlier assessment with the same reference number. Confirm this is a repeat
   and not a new assessment.
3. Decide with the owner: pay in full, or contest.
4. If the decision is to pay, check the amount and the payment account against the
   registry before anything is sent. Payment is approved by the owner only.
5. If the decision is to contest, the response must arrive before the deadline printed on
   the letter. Missing it closes the file.
6. Record the decision in the ledger with the reference number.

## If you cannot settle it

A final notice always ends with a human decision. Do not file it, and do not pay it
without the owner's approval.
