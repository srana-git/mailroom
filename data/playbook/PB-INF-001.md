## When this applies

The letter informs, confirms or acknowledges something and asks for nothing. Typical
wording is "for information only", "no action required", "aucune action requise", or an
acknowledgement of something we already sent.

## Steps

1. Read the whole letter once to confirm it asks for nothing. A paragraph in the middle
   sometimes does.
2. Check for a hidden deadline: an appeal period, a renewal date, a rate change date.
3. If there is a date, diary it even when no action is due now.
4. File the letter under the account or the file it refers to.
5. Record nothing in the ledger.

## If you cannot settle it

If the letter carries a period that is running, such as a right to object, it is not
information only and belongs with the procedure it names.
