## When this applies

A supplier announces new prices, a new minimum order, or a change to delivery terms.
The letter is a notice.

## Steps

1. Note the effective date of the change.
2. List the items we actually buy and work out the annual effect.
3. Check whether we hold a contract that fixes the old price for a period. The notice
   does not override a signed contract.
4. Send the working to the owner before the effective date.
5. Update the item costs in the purchasing system on the effective date.
6. File the notice with the supplier file.

## If you cannot settle it

An increase above ten percent on an item we buy weekly is worth a conversation with the
owner before the effective date.
