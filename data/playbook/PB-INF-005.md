## When this applies

A bank or a lender announces a change to a rate, a fee or the terms of an account or a
loan. The letter is a notice of a change that takes effect on its own.

## Steps

1. Identify the account or the loan the change applies to.
2. Work out the annual cost or saving of the change.
3. Check the change against the signed agreement. A fee the agreement does not allow is
   worth a written question.
4. Tell the owner if the annual effect is more than a few hundred dollars.
5. File the notice with the loan or the account.

## If you cannot settle it

If the notice changes when payments are taken, escalate. A moved payment date creates a
missed payment.
