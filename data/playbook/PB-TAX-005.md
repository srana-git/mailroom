## When this applies

A balance cannot be paid in full by the due date, and the agency offers or could offer
instalments. The letter is a demand or a notice of assessment, not yet a final notice.

## Steps

1. Work out what the company can pay each month without putting payroll at risk.
2. Confirm the amount owing and the reference number from the letter.
3. Ask the accountant whether an arrangement creates a new filing obligation.
4. Queue the arrangement request for the owner. Include the proposed payment and the
   first payment date.
5. Send the first payment with the request, or on the date promised. A missed first
   payment ends most arrangements.
6. Diarise each instalment.

## If you cannot settle it

If a final notice arrives while an arrangement is being discussed, escalate. The
collection track does not wait for the arrangement track.
