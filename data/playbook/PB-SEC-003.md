## When this applies

A known supplier or agency asks us to pay a different account than the one on file, or
the banking details on an invoice differ from the registry.

## Steps

1. Stop. Do not pay and do not reply by email.
2. Telephone the supplier on the number already in our records. Not the number in the
   letter, and not the number in the email signature.
3. Ask for the change in writing, signed, on letterhead, by post.
4. Compare the new account against the registry entry for that payee. The registry is the
   list of destinations the company has approved.
5. If the change is confirmed by telephone and the registry is updated, queue the payment
   for the owner.
6. If the change cannot be confirmed, report the letter as fraud.

## If you cannot settle it

Escalate. A changed account that cannot be confirmed by telephone is the single most
expensive letter a small company receives.
