## When this applies

The agency acknowledges receipt of an information return, or tells us it has processed a
filing without any balance owing.

## Steps

1. Confirm the period and the account number on the acknowledgement.
2. Match it against the return we sent.
3. File the acknowledgement with the return for that period.
4. If the acknowledgement names a period we did not file, escalate.

## If you cannot settle it

An acknowledgement for an unknown period means another party is filing under our account
number. Escalate the same week.
