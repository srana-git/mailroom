## When this applies

The letter charges a penalty or interest for a late remittance, and we believe the
lateness had a reason the agency accepts, such as a bank failure or a documented
illness.

## Steps

1. Confirm the period, the amount and the reason the agency gives for the charge.
2. Check whether the payment was in fact late. A payment made on the date shown as late
   is worth a written challenge.
3. Gather the evidence for the reason. One document beats a long explanation.
4. Queue the request for relief for the owner, with the evidence attached.
5. If the amount is small and the evidence is thin, pay it and record the decision. The
   cost of the argument can exceed the penalty.
6. Note the outcome against the account.

## If you cannot settle it

Repeat penalties across periods are a pattern. Escalate rather than paying each one
quietly.
